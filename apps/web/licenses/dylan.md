# Dylan teacher avatars

The teacher portraits in `public/teachers/dylan-v1/` are generated with
[DiceBear Dylan](https://www.dicebear.com/styles/dylan/) 10.6.0, a remix of
[Dylan! The Avatar Generator](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1356575240759683500)
by [Natalia Spivak](https://nataspvk.tilda.ws/), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Speakwow customized the hairstyle, expression, facial hair, skin/hair colors and
background for each fictional teacher. The original artwork remains under CC BY 4.0;
the repository's MIT license does not replace that license. Retain this attribution
when redistributing the portraits. DiceBear's generation library is MIT-licensed.

Design source: `assets/teachers/dylan.json`. Regenerate with `pnpm avatars:generate`.
Generated SVGs retain DiceBear's embedded creator/license metadata. Bump `dylan-v1`
in the generator, component and cache headers when changing published artwork.
