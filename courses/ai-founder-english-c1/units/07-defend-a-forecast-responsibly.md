# Unit 7 · Defend a Forecast Responsibly

## Level alignment

Target C1; focus: Formal discussion; propositional precision. The primary language evidence comes from a sustained independent interaction, not task vocabulary, business success or a prepared speech. See the [alignment review](../../../docs/AI_FOUNDER_LEVEL_ALIGNMENT.md) for task controls and the distinction between a teaching target and a proficiency result.

Use related original viewpoints with clear attribution; allow rereading. Explain unfamiliar business concepts before assessment.

Look for sustained organization, spontaneous reformulation, precise qualification and appropriate register across follow-up turns.

Do not require native-like accent or specialized business expertise. Micro-drills are preparation, not evidence of C1 proficiency.

## Teaching and assessment

Use original case facts with a consistent interpretation available to the tutor. Invite the learner to test ambiguous meanings; do not reward guessing. If teaching help is needed during a check, pause it and use an equivalent new variant later.

Writing requires a typed answer; dictation answers stay hidden until submission. A cafe image is only a context cue. Keep text materials within 2,400 characters; never pretend an unseen deck, live meeting or waveform is available.

Revisit an earlier expression at the next meeting and use a changed context in a later assessment. Save completion before asking whether to continue, rest or chat. Review status remains draft until learner evidence and rating calibration exist.
