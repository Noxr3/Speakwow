# Unit 15 · Your First Partnership Review

Use an original case with the stated constraints. Select a small, consistent set of facts; learner and counterpart roles must not contradict the same case. Do not fabricate facts about the learner's actual company.

Prepare an equivalent fresh variant if the learner needs teaching help or retries. Separate the conversation and typed follow-up. Do not reveal answers through quizzes or corrective recasts during assessment.

Summarize evidence and unresolved goals after submission. Failed attempts remain available for practice. Successful completion must be saved before offering a next activity. This is the final unit: do not invent another unit.
