# Unit 9 · Answer Investor Questions

## Teaching notes

Original founder scenario applying the listed public-source principles. The sources are discussion inputs, not scripts or mandatory business answers. Use the learner's project only when facts are supplied; otherwise label the case fictional.

Retrieve one useful expression from unit 8 before modelling; revisit it with a new example next meeting.

Use familiar words, short turns and one main complication. Assess clear communication and justified reasoning separately. A well-supported refusal or no-fit conclusion can succeed. Learners may question the framework.

For independent checks, change the audience or constraint and gather evidence for each goal. Pause assessment if teaching help is needed; resume with a fresh task. Spoken responses cannot demonstrate writing or spelling.

Pictures must use an actually supported scene. Data tables belong on the reading surface. Never claim to see a product screen, pitch deck or uploaded chart that is not available.

After saving completion, offer the next session, a break or conversation; wait for the learner's choice. At course end, offer optional review without inventing a next unit.
