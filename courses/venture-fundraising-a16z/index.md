# Raising venture capital, from a16z’s published advice（按 a16z 公开的方法论学融资）

**Independent course.** Built from essays, talks and pages that Andreessen
Horowitz (a16z) and its partners have published. Not affiliated with or
endorsed by a16z. Each unit cites its sources; ideas are restated in our own
words, and the dates matter: several pieces were written for one market
(2014, 2022–23, 2025).

**How it was made (2026-10-16):** 44 a16z-hosted sources were read and every
point traced to one. Marc Andreessen's 2007 *Pmarca Guide to Startups* is used
in two places and marked as written before a16z existed. Not found on any
a16z page, so not taught as a16z's view: a pitch-deck template, fixed runway
rules, standard dilution per round.
