points:
  - a16z says it bets on the size of a team’s and idea’s strengths, not on their lack of weaknesses
  - Most investors weigh market, product and team, and differ in the order they rank them
  - "Founder/market fit: founders with deep knowledge of the market they enter (Dixon, via a summary a16z published)"
  - Investors see layers of risk; a founder removes them one by one with evidence before raising
  - At team stage the team and market carry the pitch; at product stage show real signs of product/market fit
  - Horowitz explains why a16z prefers founders who stay on as CEO
  - Investors fund painkillers (a big improvement on the status quo), not vitamins
activities:
  - Explain in two minutes why you are the right founders for this market; the teacher asks follow-ups like a partner
  - List your company’s risk layers together and mark which ones you have evidence against
  - Tell the market-size story for your idea without quoting a TAM figure
pitfalls:
  - Defending every weakness instead of showing an outsized strength
  - A top-down TAM built to look large rather than sized from real customers
  - Claiming product/market fit with pilots, partnership talks or customer quotes
