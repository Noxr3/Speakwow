points:
  - "Look past the headline price: multiples, participation, seniority and ratchets can cancel it out"
  - A clean deal is standard preferred stock with a 1x non-participating preference
  - Compare a structured offer with a clean offer at a lower price, outcome by outcome
  - SAFEs are simple, but rolling closes can hide dilution; track the fully converted cap table
  - Do not name a valuation too early or anchor on your last round
  - A clean down round dilutes more but keeps the cap table simple; tell employees the truth
  - Terms agreed in one round usually become the starting point for the next
activities:
  - The teacher reads you two offers; you choose one and explain the outcome for founders at a small exit
  - Explain a 1x non-participating preference to the teacher as if they were a new employee
  - Add up three SAFEs together and see what you have really sold
pitfalls:
  - Chasing the highest headline valuation
  - Losing track of dilution across SAFEs
  - Accepting structure without modelling weaker outcomes
