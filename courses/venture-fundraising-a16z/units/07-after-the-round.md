points:
  - Send the board package days ahead and spend most of the meeting on future decisions
  - Report bad news as plainly as good, with consistent KPIs
  - Choose board members who coach, help recruit and respond quickly; check references
  - Avoid a board of only investors; add independents to fill real gaps
  - Keep operating cash safe and liquid, with a board-approved policy
  - If the valuation falls, tell employees the truth and fix option prices where needed
activities:
  - Draft the agenda of your next board meeting together
  - "Role play: tell your lead investor you will miss the quarter’s target"
  - Describe the independent director your company needs next, and why
pitfalls:
  - Board meetings that only look backward at numbers
  - Hiding bad news until the next meeting
  - Picking investors on price alone and getting an adversarial board
