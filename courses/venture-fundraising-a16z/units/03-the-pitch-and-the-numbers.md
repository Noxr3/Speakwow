points:
  - "No official a16z deck template exists; one partner lists the minimum: thesis, vision, competition, traction, team, use of funds"
  - Count only recurring revenue as ARR, and keep bookings and GMV apart from revenue
  - Base lifetime value on profit, show paid as well as blended CAC, and separate gross from net churn
  - Downloads and registered users are vanity metrics; show engagement and cohort retention
  - Avoid cumulative charts and unlabeled or truncated axes
  - Use the same numbers in the deck and the data room, label projections, and show full cohorts
  - Do not signal more maturity than you can prove
activities:
  - Give your one-minute pitch; the teacher interrupts the way a busy partner would
  - Read out your key metrics; the teacher checks each definition and asks for the honest version
  - "Rehearse answers to three hard questions: retention, rising CAC, and use of funds"
pitfalls:
  - Calling bookings or GMV revenue
  - Cherry-picking the best cohorts
  - Projections you cannot hit, which the next round will compare against
  - Buzzwords instead of evidence
