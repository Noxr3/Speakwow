# Partnership Conversation: C2 Integrated Check

This is a task-specific assessment, not a CEFR qualification. Prepare consistent original facts and a comparable unused variant before assessing. Make instructions understandable; keep the challenge in the interaction and language rather than hidden business prerequisites.

Collect oral and typed evidence separately. Do not coach answers during the check. If help is needed, pause, teach and later change the case while preserving its language requirements. Follow the [alignment review](../../../docs/AI_FOUNDER_LEVEL_ALIGNMENT.md).

This is the last unit. Save the result and acknowledge completion; no automatic next lesson.
