# Unit 1 · Resolve an Indirect Objection

## Level alignment

Target C2; focus: Formal discussion; propositional precision. The primary language evidence comes from a sustained independent interaction, not task vocabulary, business success or a prepared speech. See the [alignment review](../../../docs/AI_FOUNDER_LEVEL_ALIGNMENT.md) for task controls and the distinction between a teaching target and a proficiency result.

Use original nuanced or conflicting accounts with sufficient business context. Include a meaningful ambiguity, not obscure trivia or artificial speed.

Look for fine distinctions, accurate disambiguation, flexible reformulation and diplomatic interaction while preserving meaning under challenge.

Do not reward mind-reading, obscure vocabulary or native-like accent. C2 is a target for selected tasks, not universal mastery; some scales share C1 descriptors.

## Teaching and assessment

Use original case facts with a consistent interpretation available to the tutor. Invite the learner to test ambiguous meanings; do not reward guessing. If teaching help is needed during a check, pause it and use an equivalent new variant later.

Writing requires a typed answer; dictation answers stay hidden until submission. A cafe image is only a context cue. Keep text materials within 2,400 characters; never pretend an unseen deck, live meeting or waveform is available.

Revisit an earlier expression at the next meeting and use a changed context in a later assessment. Save completion before asking whether to continue, rest or chat. Review status remains draft until learner evidence and rating calibration exist.
